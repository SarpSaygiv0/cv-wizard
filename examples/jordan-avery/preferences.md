# Preferences

## Headlines
- Neutral headline (master CV): "Software Engineer | Python | Go | PostgreSQL".
- Backend-heavy postings: "Backend Software Engineer · <2–3 posting keywords>".
- Platform or infrastructure postings: "Platform Engineer · AWS · Terraform · <keyword>", only when platform work is the core of the role.
- Never "Senior" unless the posting's level matches and I confirm it.

## Dealbreakers
- Relocation away from Denver, or regular on-site work outside the Denver area.
- An active US security clearance.
- On-call more often than one week in four.

## Inclusion rules
- The internship gets at most one bullet, and can go when space is tight.
- Mention Spanish only when the role involves Spanish-speaking customers or Latin American markets.
- At most one project on a one-page CV.

## Voice & style
- US English.
- CV bullets start with a strong verb; no first-person pronouns.
- State experience as "six years' experience", counted from Jul 2020, my first full-time role. Don't count the internship.

## Cover letters
- Under 300 words.
