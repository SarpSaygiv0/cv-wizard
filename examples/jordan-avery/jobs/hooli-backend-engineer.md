# Backend Engineer, Shipping Platform — Hooli

> Fictional job posting, used to produce the documents in `output/`.

**Location:** Remote (US) · **Team:** Shipping Platform

Hooli's Shipping Platform team moves millions of parcels a week for merchants on our
marketplace. We're hiring a backend engineer to help rebuild how shipment events flow
between carriers, merchants, and our fulfillment centers.

## What you'll do
- Design and build Go and Python services that ingest carrier events at high volume.
- Own services end to end: design reviews, implementation, observability, and on-call (one week in six).
- Improve the reliability of merchant-facing webhooks and APIs.
- Work with product and data teams to expose shipping data for analytics.

## What we're looking for
- 4+ years building production backend services.
- Strong experience with Go or Python.
- Experience with event streaming (Kafka, Kinesis, or Pub/Sub).
- Solid SQL and PostgreSQL experience.
- Experience operating services on AWS or GCP.

## Nice to have
- Kubernetes.
- Experience in logistics, shipping, or e-commerce.
- dbt or other analytics tooling.

US work authorization required.
